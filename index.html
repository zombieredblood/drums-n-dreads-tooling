Drums N Dreads Tooling

Back-office automation for a personal YouTube media operation

Drums N Dreads Tooling is a personal, self-hosted application used by its owner to render and upload gaming highlight videos (Counter-Strike 2 frag movies) to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not a public service and is not offered to third parties.

What it does: assembles video clips, generates thumbnails, and uploads the finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API, using the owner's own Google account.

This app is operated for personal use only. For details on how it handles data, see the Privacy Policy.
